## 1. Objectives

Presentation and Project aim at the knowledge and skills expected in course learning outcomes:
* Help learners self-study and work in groups on data engineering-related topics.
* Enable learners to develop an application that reflects the effectiveness of data engineering results.
* Support learners in improving presentation skills.

## 2. Detailed Description

### 2.1. Application Domain

The learners are asked to work on **one application domain assigned for each group**, which is unique within the scope of our course. 

* **Typical domains:** Education, healthcare, manufacturing, marketing, e-commerce, tourism industry, science, transportation, supply chain management, entertainment, etc. These domains are introduced because it is easy to approach them for business processes and datasets.
* **Key User Groups:** Learners must identify the potential key user groups they intend to support.
* **Business Requirements:** Determine business requirements that require data prepared by data engineering (e.g., data analysis, data science, data mining, business intelligence systems, decision support systems, etc.).

---

### 2.2. Topic: Build a System Where Your Data Engineering Solution Is Proved Successful (in a Specific Application Domain)

The topic requires learners to conduct data engineering such that the resulting data can be utilized specifically for users in an application domain.

#### Three Main Parts of the Project Product:
1. **Business and related data** in an application domain.
2. **Data engineering** for the business requirements.
3. **Application development** to bring the results to the real world.

#### Technology Scope & Choices:
In the scope of the course, **data management and processing aspects are focused**. Each group needs to clarify and work with several technologies selected for data management and processing:
* **a. Data management:** Redshift, Hive, Synapse Analytics, Cassandra, BigQuery (BigLake), CouchBase, Databricks, Neo4j, RavenDB, DynamoDB, Oracle data lakehouse, etc.
* **b. Data processing:** Flink, Spark Streaming, Hadoop MapReduce, Storm, Kafka Streams, etc.

#### Group Requirement Rules:
* **Business Requirements count:** At least $2 \times n$ business requirements must be defined to utilize data from data engineering, where $n$ is the number of group members.
* **Alignment:** The business requirements need to show support for the business objectives of the enterprise in the uniquely selected application domain.
* **Rationales & Evaluation:** Provide the rationales behind data engineering's support for those requirements and evaluate the selected technologies. Alternative solutions might be considered for benchmarking from different perspectives, especially in the Big Data context.

---

### Specific Requirements for Deliverables

#### i. Presentation
* Introduce the context of the assigned presentation topic.
* Present the details of the presentation topic.
* Present the presentation topic for the project.
* Introduce/present other related issues if needed.

#### ii. Project
* Introduce the context of the project topic.
* Introduce stakeholders (especially users), along with business requirements and data usage needs, in order to shape a data-driven application for the group's application domain.
* Present expected data sources and data characteristics that should be considered when implementing data engineering.
* Present the data engineering solution and introduce at least one alternative solution (for comparison).
* Present the technology for data management, the technology for data processing, and approaches to leveraging these technologies for the application.
* Develop the application for the project topic.
* Evaluate the data engineering solution and data-driven application based on:
  * Data correctness after data engineering is performed.
  * Performance of the data engineering solution.
  * Effectiveness of supporting data exploitation through the application.
* Conclusions about the project.

### 2.3. Change Policy

* **Group changes:** Members of different groups may switch groups after the announcement, following discussion with the instructor. The condition for any change is mutual agreement among members of both original and new groups.
* **Presentation schedule changes:** A group may change the presentation schedule as published in the in-class presentation timetable. The condition for the change is swapping presentation slots with another group in the new week that the group wishes to move to.
